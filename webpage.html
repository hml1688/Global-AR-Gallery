Global AR Museum

Step into a miniature global art museum anytime, anywhere. Immerse yourself in artworks from different cultures and eras, side-by-side, to discover fascinating connections. Or experience exhibitions dynamically themed by your local weather—transforming your immediate environment into an interactive augmented reality gallery.

Purpose & Vision

Global AR Museum explores new ways of engaging with open-access cultural data through immersive AR experiences. By showcasing representative artworks from five major regions in a shared virtual space, it encourages cross-cultural exploration and artistic comparison across time and geography.

Through its innovative "Weather Gallery" feature, the project also demonstrates how environmental data, such as real-time weather, can dynamically shape exhibition themes.

Curated Global Content

Queries Harvard Art Museum & V&A Museum APIs to display region‑specific artworks for user‑selected timeframes.

Weather‑Responsive Themes

Integrates real‑time OpenWeather data to adapt exhibitions—vibrant sunlight-themed pieces under clear skies, dramatic artworks during storms, and more.

Embodied Navigation

Place the miniature gallery in your space, walk through it physically, or pinch‑zoom when space is limited.

How to Use

  1. Install the APK on an AR-Core compatible Android device.
  2. Open the app and grant camera & location permissions.
  3. Choose your preferred experience on the main screen:
    • Explore Gallery: Select a region (e.g., Europe) and time period (e.g., 1900–1950), then tap Generate Gallery.
    • Weather Gallery: Confirm your current weather conditions, then tap Enter Gallery to generate an environment-responsive exhibition.
  4. Point your phone at a flat surface and tap to place the miniature museum.
  5. Wait briefly while artworks load, then walk around to explore, tap on artworks for details, or refresh to see different selections.
